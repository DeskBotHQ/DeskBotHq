# DeskBot 🤖

**A bot trading Kalshi's 15-minute markets with real money, streamed live 24/7. Every win and every loss is on screen.**

- 📺 **Watch it live:** [kick.com/deskbothq](https://kick.com/deskbothq)
- 📣 **Every call as it settles:** [t.me/deskbothq](https://t.me/deskbothq)
- 🐦 **X:** [@DeskbotKalshi](https://x.com/DeskbotKalshi)
- 🛠 **Desktop app + free tools:** [deskbot.store](https://deskbot.store/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile)

### What's on the stream
- Countdown to the next 15-minute close
- Today's record and the win rate needed to break even at the prices paid
- The last 10 calls, wins and losses, straight from the bot's own trade ledger

### Open source
- [**kalshi-fee-calc**](https://github.com/DeskBotHQ/kalshi-fee-calc): Kalshi fee and break-even win-rate calculator. Python, zero dependencies, reproduces Kalshi's published fee table.
